Thu 1 Oct

In-person tour with Nick (Compass) through Rancho Del Oro, Oceanside. The notes describe 8 homes in about 2.5 hours, all close together. John's showing schedule has 9 Oceanside addresses. View, privacy (no one looking in), and outdoor space came first. A good kitchen matters to Maya. Also wanted: a walk-in closet, and a fourth flex room for an office or playroom. Solar and a vegetable garden are nice-to-haves. A gated community is preferred, and a cul-de-sac or quiet street is strongly preferred.

  • Top three: view, privacy, and outdoor space.
  • A good kitchen matters to Maya. A walk-in closet. A fourth flex room for an office or playroom.
  • Nice-to-haves: solar and a vegetable garden. Gated community preferred. Cul-de-sac or a quiet street, strongly.
  • Windows: check for fog and age. A full-house replacement is about $25-30K.
  • AC is essential inland.
  • A tankless water heater is ideal. An old unit is about $1,500 to replace.
  • Read solar lease contracts carefully. Some are predatory.
  • HOA on this tour ran about $140-200/mo. Confirm what that fee covers.
  • Single-family homes under $1M are getting hard to find. The market is a bit frozen, so buyers have more room on price.
  • Nick's tip: put the MLS photos into Claude and ask it to decorate them for layout ideas.
  • The showing schedule has 9 stops. 1861 Avenida Segovia was on it and had no tour writeup.
